Here we will show you what a Div tag(Division Tag) is used for.

Div Tag

A div tag doesnt actually have work of its own.
It is a block element which means they take a whole block area regardless of if they need it or not

Span Tag

Works same as a Div tag the only diffrence is that its is an Inline tag.
Which means it takes only the area it requires
